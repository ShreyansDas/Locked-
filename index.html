RUNNER

Scatter during the Hunter's 10-second freeze. Every 25 seconds a ping shows the Hunters where you are. Crouch (C) behind crates to stay unseen. Walk into crates, racks, containers, cars or buildings to climb them; walls, hedges and trees block you.

HUNTER

Touch a Runner to tag them. You then have 2.5 seconds to press L within reach to lock them. Get 3 locks to trigger Proper Chewing Gum.

CHEWING GUM

Roles flip. Runners chase the Hunter and touch them to be safe. The last Runner to tag the Hunter becomes the next Hunter.

POWER-UPS

Run through the spinning tokens; both roles can use all three. Sprint: faster for 6 s. Radar: Hunters reveal every Runner, Runners see the Hunters on the minimap. Smoke: almost invisible for 8 s.
